7) INTERFACE RULE TO HUB
The fixed shaft stack must not be used to justify a hub shape that blocks bearing insertion.
The lower washer and collar are fixed-shaft hardware.
They are real and must be cleared in the assembled system.
But that clearance must NOT be achieved by creating an impossible lower insertion path for the lower bearing in the main hub body.

Therefore:
- lower fixed-hardware clearance is real
- bearing insertion from the lower end must remain assembly-logical
- outer-ring seat ownership remains with the hub
- inner-ring hardware ownership remains with the fixed stack

8) METROLOGY RULE
Maintain these relationships:
- magnet face to encoder face = 1.5 mm air gap
- encoder PCB back to encoder mount surface logic remains controlled by the master
- encoder mount surface logic must remain consistent with the controller disk bottom chain
- no casual change is allowed in this metrology stack without updating the master first

Also preserve these sanity relationships from the master:
- `CHK_EncoderAirGapError` should remain zero by construction
- `CHK_EncoderMountSurfaceEqualsDiskBottom` should remain consistent with the controlled offset chain
- `CHK_NoShaftThroughHoleGap` must remain positive
- magnet-to-steel clearance checks must remain valid
