AUTHORITY MIRROR REVIEW / AUDIT — v2.00
Project: Reaction_Wheel_Lab_Project
Status: Controlled mirror audit after portable-lab base / wheel-safety master update
Intent: Keep the locked v2.00 stack synchronized after updating the master for portable lab safety, guarded wheel operation, and removable ballast support

1) PURPOSE
This audit records the controlled mirror state that must now match the current
`Master_Skeleton_Equations_v2.00.txt`.

The current controlled objective is now twofold:
- preserve the existing fixed-stack protection and serviceability logic
- strengthen the portable lab release by improving base stability, guarded wheel operation, and reversible high-stability upgrades

2) WHAT THIS AUDIT NOW RECOGNIZES AS AUTHORITATIVE
The current v2.00 master contains the following active controlled semantics:
- `LDG_*` = Lower-Deck Labyrinth Guard
- `CDG_*` = Upper Removable Rotation-to-Fixed Guard
- `GB_*` portable-base extensions for feet and removable ballast
- `WHG_*` wheel-guard interface family
- portable operating-policy limits tied to wheel speed and support condition

These are all part of the active controlled v2.00 semantics.

3) CONTROLLED DESIGN INTENT — FIXED-STACK PROTECTION
The fixed-stack protection concept remains:
- two separable guards
- lower guard mounted on `Lower_Hardware_Deck`
- upper guard owned by `Controller_Disk` but realized as a removable part
- both guards are non-structural
- both guards are lightweight / 3D-print-preferred
- both guards participate in a labyrinth relationship around the fixed stack

This remains a protection / containment / routing-discipline architecture only.
It is not a deck-stiffness architecture and not a payload support architecture.

- lower-deck-mounted
- non-structural

6) IMPORTANT CHANGE IN GOVERNING LOGIC FOR FIXED-STACK PROTECTION
This audit continues to recognize that the upper guard is no longer governed as a simple
short lip limited only by the fixed-stack tip.

The master explicitly disables that legacy interpretation through:
- `CDG_LegacyFixedTipLimiterDisabled = 1`

The current controlled intent remains overlap-driven labyrinth protection.
The key governing logic includes:
- `CDG_DesiredAxialOverlapWithLDG`
- `CDG_LDG_TotalReach`
- `CDG_RequiredTotalReachForOverlap`
- `CDG_RequiredSkirtLengthForOverlap`
- `CHK_CDG_LDG_RadialNestClearPerSide`
- `CHK_CDG_LDG_AxialOverlap`

7) WHAT THIS MEANS PHYSICALLY FOR FIXED-STACK PROTECTION
The current authority is not:
- “short upper lip only”

The current authority is:
- lower guard + upper guard together provide protection around the fixed stack
- radial nesting must remain positive
- axial overlap must remain positive / intentional
- guards must not become structural members

8) FIXED-STACK OWNERSHIP REMAINS FROZEN
This audit does NOT move ownership away from the fixed-stack subsystem.
The following remain protected and must not be casually changed:
- shaft planes
- bearing planes
- magnet face and seat chain
- encoder face / PCB / mount surface chain
- controller-disk cavity baseline
- lower utility deck locked height unless deliberately revised upstream

9) WIRING / SERVICE-SECTOR DISCIPLINE
The fixed-stack protection concept must remain compatible with the current
service-sector logic already present in the master:
- primary service sector = 225 deg
- encoder bundle exits through the primary sector discipline
- primary bundle must not cross the protected center

Therefore:
- upper guard service slot must align with the service sector
- lower guard service slot must align with the service sector
- no closed 360-degree guard interpretation is acceptable if it blocks serviceability

10) WHAT THIS AUDIT REJECTS IN THE FIXED-STACK REGION
The current controlled audit rejects the following interpretations:
- upper guard as a purely integral short disk lip only
- lower and upper guards treated as structural deck spacers
- guards used to carry heavy payloads
- guards used as battery / electronics support decks
- any interpretation that blocks encoder service routing through the service sector
- any interpretation that creates contact with the fixed stack
