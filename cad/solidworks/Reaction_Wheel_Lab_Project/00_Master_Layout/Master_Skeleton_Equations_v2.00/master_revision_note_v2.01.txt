1) WHY THIS REVISION EXISTS
This revision is created to record a specific engineering clarification discovered during Bearing_Hub review:

The v2.00 hub variable family is valid as a release baseline, but its assembly intent was not stated explicitly enough to prevent a non-logical CAD interpretation.

2) WHAT WAS DISCOVERED
A one-piece main hub body cannot be interpreted with a lower-mouth restriction smaller than bearing OD on the lower bearing insertion path.
If that interpretation is used, the lower 608 bearing cannot be assembled into the hub logically.

This is not a rejection of v2.00 release intent.
It is a clarification of how the release intent must be implemented.

3) WHAT REMAINS FROZEN FROM v2.00
The following remain intentionally preserved unless explicitly changed later:
- no-shoulder hub semantics
- outer-ring-only retention
- lower utility deck geometry
- controller disk / encoder cavity geometry
- motor deck frozen baseline
- wheel subsystem baseline
- battery / utility electronics / IMU / wiring subsystem contracts
- mass and top-level stack targets

4) REVISION RULE FOR HUB INTERPRETATION
For v2.01 onward, interpret the hub as follows:
- lower bearing inserts from bottom
- upper bearing inserts from top
- main hub body provides the two bearing seats and the seat bridge
- retainers provide axial capture
- lower-clearance intent must not block bearing insertion
