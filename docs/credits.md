# Credits and rights

- **Project and teaching purpose**: Muhammed Elyamani is credited in the project documentation for the reaction-wheel teaching testbed developed in the context of EE 381 Control Systems Laboratory, Department of Electrical Engineering, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals. The testbed was intended to help students apply feedback-control concepts to a physical system.
- **Fabrication**: The mechanical designs were initially intended for CNC manufacturing. Some parts were subsequently adapted for 3D printing during fabrication; the published CAD may differ from the assembled hardware.
- **Vendor and library geometry** appears in the CAD as component models, including representations of motors, bearings, mounting hubs, and electronics. Those components retain their respective rights and terms.
- **Reuse** is subject to the applicable rights. This portfolio snapshot has no project-wide open-source license.
